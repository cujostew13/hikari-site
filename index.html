Hikari 光

A semi-minimalist, anime-inspired personal command center for Windows: calendar and classes, a summarised inbox, assignments and exams, creative projects, habits and focus, with a locally hosted AI that explains it all. Everything stays on your machine.

Windows 11Local-firstOpen source · MIT

What Hikari connects to

  • Google Calendar and Gmail, read-only, through an OAuth client you create in your own Google Cloud project. Hikari stores only a refresh token, in Windows Credential Manager, and never marks mail as read or sends anything.
  • Canvas LMS through a personal access token you create, read-only.
  • Open-Meteo for weather, using only the coordinates of a city you pick.
  • A language model running on your own computer (LM Studio or compatible). Your email and records are never sent to a cloud AI service.
